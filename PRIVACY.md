# Privacy Policy — Simmer and Simmer Lite

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

**Short version: Simmer collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- **No network access.** Neither edition opens any network connection.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**
- **What the app reads, on your computer only:** names, paths and CPU times of running processes, power source and battery level, time since your last input, whether an app plays audio (and, in the full edition, whether it has a download open). It needs these to decide which apps to tame. The values are never stored beyond short averages in memory.
- **What the app stores, on your computer only:** your rules and settings, plus simple statistics (running time, CPU time saved, apps quit). The full edition can import rules from App Tamer's settings on first launch, if present.
- **Helper (full edition only):** a small system helper that receives instructions only from the signed Simmer app. It stores nothing.

You can remove everything by deleting the app and its settings (`~/Library/Preferences/com.ip-style.simmer.plist`, or the container `~/Library/Containers/com.ip-style.simmerlite` for Simmer Lite).
