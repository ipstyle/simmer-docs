What it does

Battery first

  • Tames apps only on battery — or always, your choice
  • Optional charge-level threshold
  • Pauses when you are idle

Per-app rules

  • Efficiency cores when in background
  • CPU limit from 1 % up to 500 %
  • Stop completely while in background
  • Hide or quit after N minutes idle

Smart protection

  • Never touches essential system processes
  • Leaves apps alone while they play audio or download
  • Wakes an app the moment you bring it to front

Menu bar

  • Flame icon with live CPU usage
  • Performance and efficiency core load
  • Panel with top processes and tamed apps

Alerts

  • Notification when an app burns CPU for too long
  • Limit it with one click

Two editions

  • Simmer Lite in the App Store: efficiency cores only
  • Simmer (download): full control with a small helper

The App Store only allows sandboxed apps, and the sandbox forbids pausing or limiting other apps. Simmer Lite does what the sandbox allows; the download edition does everything.

Install

  1. Download Simmer-x.y.z.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Move Simmer.app to your Applications folder and open it. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.
  3. Optional: install the helper in Settings → General and approve it once in System Settings → General → Login Items. Without it Simmer controls only your own apps.

Do not run Simmer together with another app tamer — both would fight over the same apps.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no network access at all. Simmer reads process names and CPU times on your computer and keeps your rules in its own settings. Nothing leaves your computer.
